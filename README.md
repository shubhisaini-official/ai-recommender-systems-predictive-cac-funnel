## 📌 Executive Summary

* **Objective:** Deploy personalized AI recommender systems (benchmarking **Netflix**, **Spotify**, and **Amazon**), optimize inventory demand forecasting to reduce Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), and bridge the **Attitude-Intention-Behavior** drop-off funnel.
* **Application:** Applied to **AT Food Co.’s** fresh meal subscription recommendation engine and sentiment analysis pipeline.
* **Core Takeaway:** Retaining existing customers is significantly cheaper than acquiring new ones. AI recommender engines drive repeat purchase loyalty, while predictive analytics keeps inventory lean, protecting contribution margins and CAC efficiency.

## 📊 2. Managing the Attitude-Intention-Behavior Funnel

| Funnel Stage | Consumer Psychological State | AT Food Co. CRO Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Attitude** | Positive view on fresh pet nutrition | Educational content marketing highlighting processed kibble risks. |
| **2. Intention** | High purchase intent / Cart add | Custom caloric requirement calculator & risk-free trial box offer. |
| **3. Behavior (Conversion)** | Actual subscription purchase completed ⚠️ **GAP** | Automated checkout nudges, retargeting & free shipping waivers. |

* **The Intent-Behavior Gap:** Customers frequently express positive attitudes and purchase intent toward healthy/sustainable products but drop off before checkout. Targeted nudges and friction elimination close this gap.

---

## 📈 3. Predictive Demand Analytics & CAC Optimization

1. **Inventory Holding Cost:** Unpredictable demand surges increase storage costs, compressing profit margins and restricting customer acquisition budgets.
2. **Predictive Analytics:** AI models analyze seasonal buying patterns, local weather shifts, and pet renewal cycles to forecast stock needs accurately.
3. **Sentiment Analysis:** Continuous NLP sentiment tracking on social comments and reviews alerts growth teams to emerging friction before it impacts CAC.
